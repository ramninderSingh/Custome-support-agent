# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund

Customers may request a refund within 30 days
of the original transaction.

## Duplicate Charges

If the same subscription is charged twice for
the same billing period, the duplicate transaction
is eligible for a full refund.

## Eligibility

A refund may be automatically issued when:

- The transaction is successful.
- The transaction is less than 30 days old.
- The duplicate charge can be verified.
- The transaction has not already been refunded.

## Large Refunds

Refunds above INR 20,000 require human approval.

## Refund Processing

Once approved, refunds should be issued through
the payment system and verified afterward.
